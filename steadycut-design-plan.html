SteadyCut From clip sorter → assisted rough-cut for wedding / event footage

SHELVED Mode Startup 2026-06-24 branch chore/populate-bin-ffmpeg Snavy05/premiere-sorter

Problem Statement

Users don't want a sorter that hands back tidy bins. They want an assistant that finds the good shots and assembles an almost-finished timeline — the rough cut, minus music.

SteadyCut today finds the steadiest window of each clip, classifies shots, and exports a color-coded sequence for Premiere/DaVinci. After testing with two real wedding/event videographers (Hoang, Dat) on their own footage, the core promise is missing: output lands at ~50% accuracy and does the wrong job.

The builder feels the project is "going nowhere" because effort has gone into many independent surfaces (proxy gen, GPU fallback, dual-NLE export, live Resolve API, cross-platform builds) while the one thing that gates a paying user — shot-selection quality — has not moved. The bug count feels infinite because the scope is too wide; narrowing the surface collapses it.

Status Quo

A wedding/event job = ~100–300 clips. The videographer manually scrubs every clip, mentally tags keepers, discards shaky/empty/mis-framed takes, and hand-assembles a rough cut before real editing begins. Hours of dread per job, repeated every job.

"Sifting through all of them is really tough work."  That manual sift is the competitor — not another app.

Target User & Narrowest Wedge

User

Hoang — working wedding/event videographer, edits in Adobe Premiere on macOS, 100+ clips per job, hates the manual sift.

Wedge — a keep / cut review tool

The AI pre-sorts a job's clips by keep-worthiness and drops the user into a fast one-clip-at-a-time review (keep / cut, ~2 sec each). Approved clips assemble straight into a timeline. The AI does the 3-hour grunt work; the human does the 10-minute judgment only they can make.

Why it works

The wedge deliberately does not require the AI to be near-perfect. At 70% pre-sort quality the human cheaply fixes the rest, and the result still beats a 3-hour manual sift. As the underlying signal improves, the human does less over time — same product, silently better.

Constraints

  • Solo builder, credit-conscious.
  • Reuse: four-phase Python pipeline · bundled ffmpeg .app · YOLOv8 person/pose · NLE timeline export (FCP7 XML + live Resolve API) · desktop shell (WKWebView / WebView2).
  • Immediate scope cut (resolved): Hoang uses Premiere on macOS. Keep the Premiere FCP7-XML + macOS (WKWebView) paths; freeze the DaVinci Resolve API and Windows builds until a paying user demands them.

Approaches Considered

A  Prove the signal — minimal, do first regardless

Effort S–MRisk Low

Build a keep/cut review screen, use it to hand-label Hoang's + Dat's footage (~2–3 jobs, ~300–500 clips, under an hour of one-time labeling), then swap the objective from optical-flow stability to content signals (face presence/size, person framing, sharpness, audio energy marking moments). Score the pre-sort against the labels until keepers rank near the top.

Reuses YOLOv8, ffmpeg audio, existing window logic as a baseline to beat. Not a product by itself — it is the measuring instrument.

B  Full auto rough-cut assembler — the ideal, and the trap

Effort XLRisk High

AI watches everything, finds the good shots, assembles a near-finished timeline with no human in the loop. The dream and the largest market — but full editorial judgment at >90% is research-grade for a solo builder. From 50%, an autonomous editor is a cliff, not a step. Where 6 months disappear.

C  Editor-in-the-loop fast review → timeline — the wedge

Effort M–LRisk Med

AI pre-selects candidates with A's improved signals; the human reviews fast (keep/cut, ~2 sec/clip) and approved clips assemble into a timeline. Collapses the accuracy requirement, ships, and delivers the timeline promise without solving full automation. Reuses the whole pipeline as pre-filter and existing NLE export as the timeline writer.

Review unit (chosen): whole-clip keep/cut for v1 — cheapest thing that yields a usable timeline and a clean accuracy number. Clip + chosen-window trimming is a fast-follow once keep/cut accuracy is real.

B is explicitly deferred. Earn the way to it after C has paying users.

Success Criteria
