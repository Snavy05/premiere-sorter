Experimental

Features still in active development. They may produce unexpected results — not part of the supported launch flow.

Use at your own risk
SteadyCut launches as a stable-window finder. The features below — action detection, cut-on-action, and shot classification — are unfinished and off by default. Turn them on only to experiment.

Stable-window detection is the supported mode. Action detection uses AI pose tracking and is still being tuned.

Adds a cut or marker at the detected motion peak inside each window. Unreliable on multi-subject footage.
